FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to build a website? +
Most websites are completed in 7–14 business days from the time we receive your content and deposit. Larger projects with custom functionality may take 3–4 weeks. We'll give you a specific timeline in your proposal before any work begins.
Do I need to provide content and photos? +
Ideally, yes — you know your business best. But if you need help, we can write copy for you and source high-quality stock photography. Just let us know during the discovery call and we'll factor it into the project scope.
Will my website work on phones and tablets? +
Absolutely — every website we build is fully responsive and tested across all major devices and screen sizes. Mobile users account for over 60% of web traffic, so this is never an afterthought for us.
What if I want changes after the site launches? +
All plans include a post-launch support window (30–90 days depending on plan) for minor changes and fixes. After that, you can sign up for an ongoing maintenance plan or request changes as a one-off — we're always available.
Do you offer hosting and domain setup? +
Yes. We can handle domain registration, hosting setup, and SSL certificate installation as part of the project. We'll also walk you through how to manage your hosting account so you're never dependent on us for the basics.
How do payments work? +
We typically work on a 50/50 schedule — 50% upfront to begin work and 50% upon final approval before launch. For larger projects, we can structure payments in thirds. We accept bank transfer, card, PayPal, and Venmo.
